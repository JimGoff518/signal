# 🎮 DALLAS DOCKET: RUSH HOUR
## Official Game Concept & Art Design Document

This document outlines the world, character, and pixel-art designs for the ultimate 16-bit legal action game, **Dallas Docket: Rush Hour**. Use this guide to generate more art and expand the game!

---

### 👤 THE HERO: THE LITIGATOR
**Description:** A sharp-dressed lawyer fighting against the gridlock of the Dallas legal system. 
**Weapons & Gear:**
* **The Gavel of Justice:** Primary weapon, used for smashing red tape.
* **Briefcase of Evidence:** Used as a shield against corporate attacks.
* **Suit & Tie:** Classic blue or grey suit, perfectly tailored for 16-bit action.

**Art Style Prompting Guide for the Hero:** > "16-bit retro SNES pixel art style of a handsome male lawyer in a sharp suit. He is holding a glowing wooden gavel. Bright, colorful pixel art, dynamic action pose, retro video game aesthetic."

---

### 👾 THE VILLAINS & BOSS BATTLES

#### 1. The Mega-Motion (Level 1 Boss)
* **Concept:** A giant, angry stack of paperwork spitting paperclips and red tape.
* **Design Prompt:** "16-bit pixel art of a giant boss monster made entirely out of messy paperwork, legal briefs, and red tape. It has glowing angry eyes and is shooting paperclips. Retro video game boss battle screen."

#### 2. The Misinformation Machine (Level 2 Boss)
* **Concept:** A giant, evil "Tort Reform" robot that spits out "false flags" to scare the public into thinking lawsuits hurt them.
* **Design Prompt:** "16-bit pixel art of a massive, menacing corporate robot labeled 'Misinformation'. The robot is shooting out fake red flags and propaganda at a pixel-art lawyer who is defending himself with a gavel. Retro arcade fighting game style."

#### 3. The Corporate Conspiracy (Chapter 3 Boss)
* **Concept:** A shadowy, massive figure looming over the Dallas skyline, representing corporate greed and systemic red tape.
* **Design Prompt:** "16-bit pixel art teaser screen showing a tiny pixelated lawyer standing before a massive, shadowy corporate villain with glowing red eyes looming over the Dallas skyline. Text at the top reads 'CHAPTER 3: THE CORPORATE CONSPIRACY'."

### 🏆 LEVELS & SCREENS

#### The Box Art
* **Concept:** Vintage 1990s Super Nintendo game cartridge sleeve.
* **Design Prompt:** "Faded, vintage 1990s Super Nintendo game box art for a game called 'DALLAS DOCKET: RUSH HOUR'. Featuring a pixel-art lawyer jumping over traffic holding a gavel. 16-bit graphics logo, worn cardboard edges."

#### The Evidence Dash (Bonus Stage)
* **Concept:** A speed-run level where The Litigator races through the courthouse halls.
* **Design Prompt:** "16-bit pixel art video game screenshot. A lawyer in a suit running through a classic mahogany courthouse hallway. He is collecting glowing yellow evidence folders while dodging flying gavels. Retro arcade UI at the top reads 'BONUS STAGE' and 'TIME: 0:30'."

#### The Victory Screen (Level Complete)
* **Concept:** Celebrating the defeat of the Misinformation Machine.
* **Design Prompt:** "16-bit pixel art of a lawyer standing triumphantly on a podium in a courtroom, holding his gavel high. Pixelated confetti is falling. A crowd of pixelated people are cheering. UI Text reads 'LEVEL COMPLETE', 'LIES DEBUNKED: 100%', and 'PUBLIC TRUST: RESTORED'."

### 💡 TIPS FOR GENERATING MORE ART
1.  **Keep the style consistent:** Always include keywords like `16-bit pixel art`, `SNES style`, `retro arcade`, and `pixelated UI`.
2.  **Add UI Elements:** Ask for health bars, score counters, or bold retro text to make it look like a real screenshot.
3.  **Expand the Lore:** Need a new boss? Think of common lawyer struggles (e.g., The Broken Elevator, The Billable Hour Ghost, The Overturned Objection) and turn them into monsters!
